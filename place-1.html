Location Overview

The Smokey Hollow Mill Site encompasses the crumbling stone foundation and timber frame remnants of a 19th-century industrial flour and saw mill. Situated deep within the Grindstone Creek ravine floor along the Bruce Trail network, this spot offers a glimpse into early Waterdown industry before power shifted away from waterwheels.

Over decades of natural weathering and erosion from the creek, several masonry walls have collapsed, creating an overgrown pocket of structural decay surrounded by heavy forest canopy.

Known Hazards

  • Unstable masonry walls prone to loose falling stone.
  • Slippery wet rock faces along the creek bed.
  • Hidden sudden drop-offs and uneven foundation pits concealed by brush.
  • High water levels and flash flow risk during heavy rain.
